# PMP ECO in effect for October 2026 (accessed 2026-10-09)

**Applies:** PMP Examination Content Outline - July 2026 (PMI primary PDF). Replaced the 2021 ECO for exams from 9 Jul 2026 (date from secondary source, see Uncertain).

## Summary
| Domain | Weight | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| I People | 33% | 8 |
| II Process | 41% | 10 |
| III Business Environment | 26% | 8 |
| Total | 100% | 26 |

Exam format (PDF): 180 questions (170 scored + 10 pretest), 240 minutes; MCQ, multiple response, matching, hotspot, limited fill-in, data and case-study questions.

## Tasks (PMI PDF)
### I People - 33%
1. Develop a common vision
2. Manage conflicts
3. Lead the project team
4. Engage stakeholders
5. Align stakeholder expectations
6. Manage stakeholder expectations
7. Help ensure knowledge transfer
8. Plan and manage communication

### II Process - 41%
1. Develop an integrated project management plan and plan delivery
2. Develop and manage project scope
3. Help ensure value-based delivery
4. Plan and manage resources
5. Plan and manage procurement
6. Plan and manage finance
7. Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables
8. Plan and manage schedule
9. Evaluate project status
10. Manage project closure

### III Business Environment - 26%
1. Define and establish project governance
2. Plan and manage project compliance
3. Manage and control changes
4. Remove impediments and manage issues
5. Plan and manage risk
6. Continuous improvement
7. Support organizational change
8. Evaluate external business environment changes

(Enablers/bullets under each task are in the PDF, not copied here.)

## Approach split (PDF, verbatim idea)
Approx. 40% predictive; remaining 60% split between adaptive/agile and hybrid. Spread across all domains, not tied to a domain/task. Exact numbers vary by form. No agile-vs-hybrid sub-split stated.
